## 3. Mesh geometry [corpus, derived from the .obj — 805 verts / 960 tris, two coincident sheets of 480]

Flat tapered annulus (thickness 0.1, plane XY before orientation). Re-measured 2026-08-02 with ±4°
windows at every 30° of |angle| — the first pass used wide-bin min/max, which overstated the tail
band (±120° read {47..146} against a true {67.3..132.5}) and mis-anchored u at the seam:
- Full 360° sweep, symmetric in ±angle (the ± windows agree exactly). Radial band vs |angle|
  (deg → {inner..outer}): 0:{21.2..169.0}, 30:{23.4..165.4}, 60:{30.0..156.8}, 90:{44.1..147.1},
  120:{67.3..132.5}, 150:{88.5..117.4}, 180:{98.6..101.2}. Bounds X −103.9..168.9, Y ±147.5;
  single-sheet XY area 52,995.
- **UV: u runs ALONG the arc, counterclockwise, covering the FULL 360°**: u=0 at −180° and u=1
  back at +180° (the seam carries both 0 and ~1). The mapping is NON-uniform (arc-length-like) —
  mean u per 30° station from −180° CCW: 0, 0.1031, 0.2092, 0.3064, 0.3666, 0.4263, 0.5, 0.5737,
  0.6334, 0.6936, 0.7908, 0.8969, 1 (symmetrized; the ± station pairs agree within the uv
  quantization, 0.0015). The texture belly u 0.25..0.75 therefore spans ~215° of arc, not 180°.
  **v runs ACROSS the band: v=0 = OUTER edge, v=1 = INNER edge**. uv0 covers 0..1 fully.
- This is the fact that fixes "starts from the wrong end": the material's `offset` dynamic
  param pans along **u**, so sweep start/end/direction are determined by u's CCW winding plus
  the per-layer offset curve sign (§5), not by anything in the emitter.

Vector-from-Curve scale; integrating it stepwise would make position depend on the frame cadence and
would drift between the GPU `.ush` and the C++ mirror. The piecewise-linear curve integrates exactly,
so both sides produce identical positions at any Age.

### 8.1 New capability — row-level renderer overrides

`ck::particles::FCk_ParticlesRendererSpec` + a `RendererOverrides` view on
`FCk_ParticlesTemplateSpec`. A cadence row may declare extra VisTag-gated renderers of two kinds:

- **`Mesh`** — one named generated mesh (`SM_CkParticles_<MeshName>`) drawn with one named CkUsf look
  (`M_CkUsf_Look_<LookName>`), bound through `bOverrideMaterials` + `OverrideMaterials[0].ExplicitMat`.
- **`VelocityAlignedSprite`** — a velocity-aligned sprite renderer drawn with a named look.

The builder emits them for that row's template **only**, so the shared 0–4 renderer set stays
behavior-agnostic and no other template gains a renderer it never asked for.

VisTags allocated for this recreation, continuing after the shared set:

| VisTag | Kind | Mesh | Look | Source emitter |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Mesh | `Crescent` | `SlashDisAdd01` | Slash_01 |
| 6 | Mesh | `Crescent` | `SlashDisAdd02` | Slash_02 |
| 7 | Mesh | `Crescent` | `SlashDisAdd04` | Slash_03 (the source's own naming skew) |
| 8 | Mesh | `Crescent` | `WindDisAdd02` | Slash_Wind |
| 9 | VelocityAlignedSprite | — | `PartDisAdd04` | Sparkles_01 |

**Why the look is bound on the renderer rather than through `User.SpriteMaterial`.** One user
parameter cannot carry five different materials. `Get_BehaviorLookName(7)` therefore stays
`NAME_None` — the single-look binding path is unchanged and behavior 17 remains its only user.

**The roster-wide VisTag ceiling is derived, not restated.** `ck::particles::Get_RosterVisTag_Max()`
walks the cadence table's renderer overrides on top of `SharedRendererVisTag_Max`. The roster test
reads it instead of the literal `4` it used to hardcode, so a future row-declared renderer cannot
quietly widen a magic number in a test file.

### 8.2 The crescent mesh

`SM_CkParticles_Crescent`, MeshDescription-built like the rest of the carrier set: a flat annulus
sweeping the full 360°, radial band interpolated piecewise-linearly between §3's seven measured
knots and symmetric in ±angle. 3 band segments × 160 arc segments = **960 triangles**, the source
mesh's own triangle count.

UV follows §3 exactly: `u` runs ALONG the arc counter-clockwise over the FULL 360° — u=0 at −180°,
u=1 back at +180° — through §3's measured 30°-station table (arc-length-like, not linear in angle:
the texture belly u 0.25..0.75 spans ~215° of arc); `v` runs ACROSS the band, 0 = OUTER edge,
1 = INNER. The material's `offset` channel pans along u and wraps through the seam exactly as it
does on the source mesh. **This UV, plus the per-layer `offset` curve sign, is what fixes "starts
from the wrong end"** — nothing in the emitter decides sweep direction.

## 13. Confirmed fidelity differences or intentional deviations

**The visual gate in §12 has NOT been executed.** Everything below is either a *known* difference
derived from the source data, or *unverified*. The effect is source-verified, not visually verified.

### Known differences — deliberate

1. **Procedural textures instead of the hand paints.** Narrowed by the 2026-08-01 spectral pass:
   the bakes now run at the pack's own 512² (resolution matched) and each enriched stand-in lands
   within ~20% relative of its source on octave-band energy, structure-tensor anisotropy, 10-90
   edge rise and luminance percentile stretch (verified via a Python mirror of the bake formulas;
   §7). The remaining gap is hand-paint *idiosyncrasy* — individual brush strokes and their
   placement, which no analytic form reproduces — plus three measured residuals: WindBand's
   u-profile ridge count (19 vs 11 — the fine texture that carries its 8-16 cyc band target also
   wiggles the axis profile), SparkStreak's 16-32 / 32+ cyc split (each ~40% off in opposite
   directions; their sum matches within 5%), and TileNoise's v-axis mean-profile ridge count
   (7 vs 13-17, a realization artifact of near-flat axis means). Still a maintainer decision
   (§6.5), not an oversight.
2. **Local space, not world space.** The source's five emitters are world-space; the CkParticles
   template is local-space because self-driving behaviors write absolute positions and would collapse
   onto the world origin otherwise. Visible only if the spawning actor MOVES during the 0.5 s life —
   the source's particles would stay behind, the recreation's follow. Noted, not fixed.
3. **`DepthFade` omitted.** `Opacty_DepthFade` is 20 on the slash instances and 30 on the sparks.
   CkUsf surface looks do not wire scene depth, so the soft intersection with world geometry is
   dropped. Same gap the Lightning recipe records.
4. **`GradientMap_Displacement` (0.1) and `Opacity_StepAdd` (0.1) omitted**, Slash01 only. The
   gradient chain is a no-op on this family (a white-pixel gradient map), and the opacity step is a
   0.1 bias whose contribution is not separable from `OpacityBoldness` here `[inferred]`.
5. **Mesh thickness dropped.** §3 measures the source carrier at thickness 0.1 on a shape whose radius
   reaches 169 — 0.06%. The crescent is built as a single flat sheet and the looks are `_TwoSided`,
   which renders identically and halves the triangle count.
6. **The ±180° bounds/table disagreement is RESOLVED (2026-08-02) — both first-pass numbers were
   bin artifacts.** The re-measure with ±4° windows (§3) puts the seam band at {98.6..101.2}; the
   rebuilt 7-knot table reproduces the source silhouette (generated X min −105.6 vs source −103.9,
   XY area within 2.3%), closing the "worth a re-measure" note this entry used to carry.
7. **`RingDissolveAdd` was repointed at the shared family shader** rather than kept as a near-copy.
   Its look name, generated-master path, parameter defaults and bindings are unchanged; it gains six
   parameters at values its own source instance resolves (`DistortIntensity` 0, unit scales), which
   are mathematically inert for it. The `offset` channel now pans along U only instead of both axes —
   also inert there, since NS_Lightning_Range drives that channel at 0.

### A/B findings — diagnosed and fixed in source (2026-08-02); regeneration + re-render pending

The first visual A/B read the recreation as "~2 extra thin circular arcs, tighter than the
original's single broad 200-280° swoosh". A three-way diagnosis (texture / dissolve math / mesh)
confirmed three independent causes; all three are fixed in source:

1. **`DissolveAdd.ush` read the dissolve channel with the wrong sense** — the primary cause. It
   thresholded the noise AT the channel value, under which Slash02's and Slash03's `dissolve = 1`
   produce mask 0 — the Brightness-40 core line and the soft glow never drew a single pixel —
   while Slash01's −0.1 → −1 curve and Wind's −0.27 sat below the noise's [0,1] range and NEVER
   eroded. The composite was exactly two ungated arcs panning 1.4 / 0.75 wrap-around revolutions:
   the "extra circular rounds". The family math is now
   `Mask = smoothstep(0, DissolveEdge, Noise + dissolve)` — the channel is signed integrity ADDED
   to the noise (+1 fully intact, −1 fully dissolved; the family's name is its math). That is the
   only reading consistent with every corpus instance: `Dissolve_Invert` is 0 on all five slash
   materials AND on Ring04, so one sense must fit all of them, and the direct-threshold sense
   contradicts §5's curves outright. Python re-simulation of all four layers under the fix:
   Slash01 visible fraction 0.37 at spawn, eroding after t=0.4 to 0.0002 by t=0.95; Slash02 and
   Slash03 visible their whole life; Wind carved by its band texture and gated by its alpha
   envelope. A dissolve of exactly 0 is unchanged by the fix, so the sparks and every other
   0-driving family user are untouched. Shared-shader impact on behavior 17 (the ring now resolves
   IN over its first 40% instead of chewing gaps out) is recorded in NS_Lightning_Range.md §13.6.
2. **`Px_SlashArc01`'s body plateau sat at 0.42 of the rim** (source: 0.60-0.65 in the central
   columns) with zero intra-band ridges, so the rim stood off a dark plateau and read as its own
   thin arc — and the 4-8 cyc v-band energy overshot the source by 56%. Reworked per §7's
   re-measure: central body/rim 0.627, the measured extra body u-falloff, the five measured
   motion-line ridges at 4.9-10.6% prominence, 4-8 cyc band 0.0554 vs the source's 0.054 (+2.6%,
   contract was ±25%), coverage 0.746 vs 0.734, modulation 0.644 vs 0.613, ring count 1 vs 1.
   `Px_SlashArc02` measured structurally faithful and is untouched.
3. **The crescent's tail was too wide and its UV squeezed the texture into 308° of arc.** The
   coarse 4-knot radial table overshot the band 1.3-2.4× past |120°| (X min −118.6 vs the source's
   −103.9; XY area 1.17×), and the angle-linear u over a 308° span compressed the texture belly to
   154° of arc against the source's ~215° — the "tighter" read. Rebuilt from §3's re-measured
   7-knot + 13-station tables: XY area ratio 1.023, X min −105.6, u-mapping mean error 0.003
   (max 0.013), belly span 214.8°.

4. **The `offset` pan WRAPPED instead of clamping** — the defect behind the "goes around twice" read,
   independent of the three above. `DissolveAdd.ush` applied neither `frac` nor a clamp, and
   `CkUsf_SampleTexture2D` is a bare `Sample`, so addressing fell through to the sampler state:
   `CkParticles_TextureGenerator::Bake()` never sets `AddressX/AddressY`, leaving the baked textures at
   `TA_Wrap` by omission. Under wrap an `offset` of −1 is an IDENTITY — Slash01 shows 69.5% of the arc
   at spawn instead of nothing, the pattern travels 1.4–1.7 revolutions across the life, and a fixed
   angular position is swept two to three times (OFF→ON front arrivals at u = 0/0.25/0.5/0.75:
   Slash01 [2,2,2,2], Slash02 [2,2,3,3], Slash03 [2,2,2,3], Wind [2,1,1,1]). The parent graph clamps
   instead — `M_VFX_DisAdd_Slash01`'s expression census is Clamp 2 / Saturate 12. The shader now
   computes the panned shape U once, samples it `saturate`d, and multiplies the shape by
   `step(0, uP) * step(uP, 1)`, which reproduces Panner+Clamp against the black-edged paints
   regardless of any texture asset's address mode. Only the shape's panned axis is clamped; the
   dissolve noise keeps its time-driven wrap. Re-modelled over all four layers against the real `Px_*`
   formulas and the measured distortion field: **t = 0 rendered visibility 0.000 everywhere**, and
   **exactly one front arrival per angular position per life** — Slash01 [1,1,1,1], Slash02 [1,1,1,1],
   Slash03 [1,1,1,1], Wind [1,1,1,1]. Two readings that look like exceptions are not: Slash01 drops to
   0 arrivals at u = 0 under its most negative distortion (that end of the arc is simply never reached
   by a partial sweep — fewer than one, never two), and Wind's centre-anchored shape is ~50% in range
   at t = 0 because its pan is only ±0.375, but its own alpha envelope starts at 0, so the rendered
   contribution is still nil.

Textures, mesh, looks and templates must be REGENERATED (§12 order: build → looks → templates) and
the visual gate re-run before any of this counts as visually verified.

## 14. Reusable lessons for future effects

1. **Cadence first, and check it against the source's OWN numbers.** Behavior 7's most visible defect
   was never its shader — it was spawning 96 particles on a 1.2 s loop against a source that bursts
   19 on a 1.0 s loop. That is a table row, and it costs nothing once you look.
2. **A multi-emitter source compresses into `Seed % N`, not into `Rand(Seed) < k` bands.** The old
   behavior used probability bands, which gives a *distribution* of layers rather than exactly one of
   each per burst — so some loops silently rendered two wind ghosts and no core slash. A modulo over
   a burst of exactly N gives the source's partition by construction.
3. **The renderer set is a shared resource; per-effect renderers belong on the cadence row.** Adding
   a fifth slash-specific renderer to the shared 0–4 band would have put it on every template. Making
   it a row declaration keeps the shared set behavior-agnostic and makes the next recreation's extra
   renderer a data edit.
4. **Derive roster bounds; never restate them in a test.** The roster test hardcoded `VisTag <= 4`.
   Widening the literal would have been the obvious move and the wrong one — the bound is now computed
   from the cadence table, so it cannot drift and cannot be silently loosened.
5. **Measure textures, then bake from the numbers.** A structure tensor, per-axis mean absolute
   difference, 32-bin profiles and zero-crossing counts are enough to characterize a VFX paint in a
   dozen numbers you can put in a recipe — durable after the corpus is gone, and legally clean.
   Validate the decoder against a texture whose appearance is already documented (`T_VFX_Ring_04`
   here) before trusting a single measurement.
6. **Watch for a fallback in your measurement code.** The first pass read "content where there is
   none" because a `rgbmax > 0.001 ? rgbmax : alpha` fallback turned every black pixel white on an
   opaque-alpha PNG. It produced a plausible, entirely wrong picture of two textures. A sanity target
   caught it in one run.
7. **Integrate velocity curves in closed form.** A Vector-from-Curve velocity scale integrates exactly
   because its keys are linear. Stepwise integration would tie position to frame cadence and guarantee
   GPU/CPU drift — the module's top anti-pattern, reached by accident.
8. **One family shader, N parameterizations.** The source ships one parent graph and six instances;
   recreating it as six shaders would have been six places to fix one bug. Extending the family entry
   point and repointing the existing look cost less than the duplicate would have.
9. **A usage flag is about which RENDERER accepts the master, not what the shader may read.** The
   mesh-particle and sprite flags are independent; a look that reads `ParticleColor` needs whichever
   one matches its renderer, and getting it wrong falls back to the default material *only in a
   packaged build*. Two looks sharing one shader and differing only in the flag is the cheapest
   possible proof that the pair is wired independently.
10. **Regeneration has an order.** Row renderers resolve their CkUsf look masters at template-build
    time, so looks must be generated BEFORE templates. A template built first is not broken loudly —
    it just carries a renderer with no material.
11. **Validate a family shader's channel sense against EVERY instance's corpus curves before
    trusting one reading.** A sense under which any instance is invisible for its whole life
    (dissolve=1 → mask 0 here) is a corpus contradiction, not a tuning problem — and with
    `Dissolve_Invert` at 0 on every instance, one sense had to fit all of them. The name was the
    math all along: Dissolve**Add** adds the channel to the noise and smoothsteps the sum at zero.
    A ten-line Python composite over the real §5 curves would have caught it before the first
    render; the expression histogram alone could not.
12. **A dynamic-offset pan in this family is a CLAMPED reveal, not a rotation.** The `offset` channel
    sweeps a shape ACROSS the carrier and off its end; it never brings the shape back around. Get that
    wrong and the symptom is specific and measurable: an offset curve starting at −1 becomes an
    identity, so the layer is fully visible at spawn instead of empty, and every angular position is
    swept twice or three times instead of once — the observed "two extra circular arcs" A/B read
    (§13's A/B item 4 carries the per-layer front-arrival counts, wrap vs clamp). The trap is that
    **nothing in the shader says "wrap"** — with no `frac` and no clamp in the HLSL, the behaviour is
    decided by an address mode the texture baker never set. Do not rely on it: clamp the panned axis in
    the shader AND mask it with `step(0, uP) * step(uP, 1)`, so the look is correct against any texture
    asset. Clamp only the axis the offset drives — a dissolve or scroll layer that is *supposed* to
    tile still needs its wrap. The cheap tell before rendering anything: count front arrivals at a few
    fixed u positions over the life under both addressing modes; one directed pass is the source's
    signature, two is wrap.
13. **Measure a mesh's UV parametrization, not just its winding and endpoints.** The crescent's u
    is arc-length-like — its belly u 0.25..0.75 covers ~215° of arc — and assuming linear-in-angle
    over a 308° span compressed every texture feature to 0.77× and moved the seam. Mean u per 30°
    station off the .obj is a dozen durable numbers, exactly like the radial table. The same
    re-measure caught that wide-bin min/max statistics overstate a tapering band: use fixed
    windows at the station angles, and check the ± halves separately before symmetrizing.
